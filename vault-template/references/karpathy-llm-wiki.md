# Karpathy on the LLM wiki

## Source

Andrej Karpathy's public posts sketching how he keeps a personal knowledge base as an LLM-compiled artifact: the editor is Obsidian, the programmer is the model, and the wiki plays the role of the code. ^[inferred] (paraphrase reconstructed from public discussion of the posts, not a verbatim archive)

## Key claims

- Raw material and compiled pages must be strictly separated — otherwise repeated rewriting drifts meaning until the vault repeats its own inventions.
- An index of one-line descriptions is what lets a model know what exists without reading everything.
- Small single-idea pages linked densely beat long article dumps — links are half the value.

## Why it entered the vault

It is the originating source of this vault's whole design — distilled into [[llm-wiki-pattern]] and combined with the constitution rationale in [[why-agents-need-a-constitution]].
