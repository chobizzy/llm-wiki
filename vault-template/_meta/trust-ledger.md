# Trust Ledger

*One line per skill run, appended by the skill itself after its deterministic gate (see AGENTS.md). Format: `- [ISO-8601Z] skill=<name> agent=<agent> gate=pass|fail notes="<one line>"`. Pass rates over this ledger justify autonomy-level transitions — reviewed by the owner, never self-promoted.*
